Trigger

Putting it together

The Catalogue of Making was due at the end of this week, so it was about laying every experiment out in order and building the first version of the language itself.

Prototype

Gesture to bitmap to Hydra

Idea. Bring it together: the three categories from Prototype 1 become the vocabulary of the language.

How. Recognised hand signs become a strip of 5×5 bitmap symbols, sorted into objects, values and motions, and the strip runs as live Hydra visuals. Signs are trained from webcam recordings or video clips.

Why the bitmap. The low-resolution, bitmap look came from my Week 11 moodboard. It influenced me a little, so I kept it. I chose Hydra over p5.js in Week 14, because a new line can change what is already running.

The catalogue

I gathered the code and assets for all thirteen experiments and built the catalogue as one scroll: a moving network that shows how the experiments connect, then each one in order, with its gesture map and code. Laid out together, every little experiment brought me further and deeper into the question itself, which is why all of them are in it.
